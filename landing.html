02 / Operating model

Own a Seat. Earn your rank.

Goldstein separates the public-facing firm from the operating app. The website explains what the firm is. Inside Goldstein HQ, a Seat becomes the user's persistent identity and the gateway to primary G-Fund activity.

The UI keeps the mechanism legible: reveal → claim → stake → rank → mint. Complexity stays behind the interface instead of dominating the first interaction.
